Capital-gains statement download instructions

Curated summary of the HDFC Mutual Fund article, verified on 2026-10-02. This is a summary, not a downloaded copy of the page.

To obtain a capital-gains statement, open the official CAMS or KFintech website and request its consolidated capital-gains report for the financial year you need. Select email delivery to receive the report at the email address registered with your investments. Complete any identity checks on the official service itself.

If you use an investment platform, sign in there, open its tax or reports section, and download the capital-gains statement for the relevant financial year. A fund house website may also offer a statement-generation service.

The report concerns realized gains and losses from mutual fund redemptions. It can be generated for a chosen period. A general account statement showing holdings is a different document.
